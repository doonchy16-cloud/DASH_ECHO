# DASH ECHO 👻

**Every attempt leaves a trace.**

DASH ECHO is being built as a local Geometry Dash replay and training studio: attempt ghosts, history, death intelligence, interactive replay timelines, playback controls, and cinematic tools.

## v0.8 — Interactive Replay Studio

DASH ECHO now has source architecture for an in-game ECHO launcher and Replay Studio with one authoritative replay cursor, play/pause, restart, frame stepping, normalized slider scrubbing, and five replay-only speeds: 0.10x, 0.25x, 0.50x, 1.00x, and 2.00x.

Replay review uses the owned v0.7 historical clip, hides the multighost fleet while Studio is open, and freezes DASH ECHO's active-attempt recorder time. Replay controls do not intentionally modify Geometry Dash physics, collision, saves, or account state.

Runtime/build verification remains intentionally deferred until v1.0.
